# ToppedOff Forever

**Never pull with a missing buff, empty reagents or a bare weapon again.**

ToppedOff Forever is a reminder addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. It watches your character and shows a small icon only when something needs topping off. **Click the icon and it's fixed.**

## What it watches

- **Buffs**: your class buffs, party buffs and blessings, weapon enhancements, food buffs, elixirs and flasks, with a warning before they run out.
- **Supplies**: food, water, bandages, potions, reagents, ammo and any item you add yourself, each with a minimum you choose.
- **Your character**: your pet, gear durability and bag space.
- **Vendors and the bank**: one click restocks what you're short on or takes it from your bank. The cost is shown first, and nothing happens until you click.

Every check can be turned on or off, and your class's checks are set up for you. Type `/topoff` for the options, or `/topoff check` to see what's being checked.

## Installation

Install it with the CurseForge app, or download the file and unzip it into your WoW: Forever `Interface\AddOns` folder. Then restart the game or type `/reload`.

## All addons by me

- **TauntMaster Forever**: one-click taunts off your healers and DPS, for tanks.
- **ToppedOff Forever**: reminds you when buffs, food, reagents, ammo or gear need topping off.
- **Outfitter Forever**: save your gear as outfits and switch between them with one click, or let them change by themselves.
- **BattleText Forever**: scrolling combat text for your hits, heals and the damage you take.
- **AuctionHouse Forever**: a bigger Auction House window, so long item names stop getting cut off.
- **GatherPath Forever**: the shortest gathering route through your GatherMate2 herb and ore nodes, on the world map and minimap.
- **ARS Forever** (Azeroth Revenue Service): see how much gold a player has made and the most they've ever held, for you and anyone you inspect.
